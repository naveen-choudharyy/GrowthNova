# GrowthNova — Professional Digital Agency Platform

> **Your Vision. Our Strategy. Your Growth.**

GrowthNova is a modern, high-performance digital agency platform built with Next.js, React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, and Framer Motion, engineered with a premium **dark glassmorphism** aesthetic.

---

## 🌟 Executive Overview

GrowthNova communicates collaborative multi-team digital excellence rather than a solo freelancer portfolio. The platform is organized around 5 dedicated, cross-functional pods:

1. **Digital Marketing Team**: Social media strategy, Instagram account management, brand positioning, audience scaling, and campaign management.
2. **Web Development Team**: High-performance business websites, portfolio showcases, e-commerce stores, custom web apps, and maintenance.
3. **Creative Design Team**: Brand identities, vector logo design systems, social media creatives, marketing graphics, and promotional banners.
4. **Video Production & Editing Team**: Viral Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, cinematic promo videos, and high-retention short-form edits.
5. **SEO & Growth Team**: Technical SEO audits, high-intent keyword clustering, on-page optimization, and organic inbound acquisition.

---

## 💎 Design System & Glassmorphism

- **Primary Background**: Deep black (`#07080a`) and dark charcoal (`#0c0e14`, `#11141e`).
- **Glass Surfaces**: `backdrop-blur-xl`, semi-transparent frosted surfaces (`bg-white/[0.03]`), with subtle glows.
- **Accents**: Electric lime (`#ccff00`, `#a3e635`) and mint green (`#10b981`).
- **Typography**: Clean, high-legibility headings and body copy with KaTeX/monospace accents for metrics.
- **Buttons**: Rounded premium glass buttons with subtle glow shadow effects (`shadow-[0_0_25px_rgba(204,255,0,0.35)]`).

## 🚀 Key Functional Features

- **Interactive Project Cost Estimator**: Dynamic slider & calculator computing estimated investment ranges based on service, complexity tier, scale, and feature count.
- **Enquiry & Lead Funnel**: Validated forms storing leads with unique reference IDs and real-time activity logging.
- **Strategy Consultation System**: Formal request flow with date/time slots, noting pending status until approved by administrators.
- **Private Founder Inbox**: Full conversation threads, unread status indicators, search/archive, and founder direct reply functionality.
- **Portfolio Management**: Complete CMS supporting 6 categories (Websites, Instagram, Branding, Video Editing, Graphic Design, Marketing Campaigns) with demo labels, video modals, and external links.
- **Dual-Storage Resilience**: Mongoose/MongoDB architecture with automatic persistent local JSON fallback (`data/db.json`), ensuring 100% crash-proof operation out-of-the-box.
